## 为什么我依然喜欢单机游戏

我喜欢单机游戏，不只是因为它好玩，也因为它能给人一段完整、安静、可以自己掌握节奏的时间。

它不像信息流那样不断把注意力切碎，而是允许我进入一个世界，慢慢理解它的规则、人物、风景和沉默。

### 为什么会想记录

很多游戏时刻并不适合马上评价。它们可能只是一段路、一句台词、一个选择，或者一个停在屏幕前的瞬间。

如果不写下来，这些感受很快就会被新的内容盖过去。所以我想把它们当作手记保存，而不是当作评分或攻略。

### 我喜欢的地方

- 沉浸感：可以暂时离开日常节奏，进入另一个有秩序的世界。
- 叙事感：好的游戏会用行动、空间和细节讲故事。
- 私密感：没有社交压力，也不需要证明自己玩得多好。

### 留下来的瞬间

有时候是一个场景，有时候是一段音乐，有时候是角色做出的选择。

这些瞬间提醒我，游戏不只是娱乐，也能留下情绪、画面和节奏。

### 留下来的东西

有些虚拟世界会显得真实，有些选择会让人犹豫，有些简单场景会留在记忆里。

我想把这些片段放在这里，像保存一张路上的照片。

### 以后还想怎么记录

我不打算把这里写成专业评测。更想记录的是：玩过什么，当时为什么喜欢，以及它留下了什么情绪。

如果某个游戏让我停下来，我就给它留一页。

## Why I Still Love Single-Player Games

I like single-player games not only because they are fun, but because they offer a complete, quiet stretch of time that I can move through at my own pace.

Unlike a feed that keeps breaking attention apart, a good game lets me enter a world and slowly understand its rules, characters, landscapes, and silence.

### Why I want to keep notes

Many game moments do not need to become instant opinions. They might be a road, a line of dialogue, a choice, or a quiet pause in front of the screen.

If I do not write them down, they disappear quickly under new content. I want to keep them as notes, not as scores or guides.

### What I like

- Immersion: stepping away from daily rhythm into another ordered world.
- Storytelling: good games tell stories through action, space, and detail.
- Privacy: no social pressure, and no need to prove how well I play.

### Moments that stay

Sometimes it is a scene, sometimes a piece of music, sometimes a choice made by a character.

These moments remind me that games are not only entertainment. They can leave behind a mood, an image, or a rhythm.

### What remains

A virtual world can feel real. A choice can create hesitation. A simple scene can stay in memory for a long time.

I want to keep these fragments here, like saving a photo from a walk.

### How I want to keep recording

I do not want this to become professional reviews. I want to record what I played, why I liked it at the time, and what feeling it left behind.

If a game makes me pause, I will leave a page for it.
