## 东京散步记录

东京适合慢慢走。它不总是把自己摆成一个巨大的景点，而是藏在车站出口、便利店灯光、街角书店和安静的住宅区里。

我喜欢没有明确目的地的散步。走到一条不熟悉的小路，停下来看看招牌、窗户、树影和人的步伐，城市会变得更具体。

### 几个片段

- 清晨的电车站，所有人都很快，但秩序并不紧张。
- 小书店里安静的纸张气味，让时间变得很慢。
- 咖啡馆靠窗的位置，适合写几行没有结论的笔记。

旅行有时不是为了收集地点，而是为了换一种速度观察生活。

## Walking Notes from Tokyo

Tokyo is a city for walking slowly. It does not always present itself as one large attraction. It often hides near station exits, convenience store lights, corner bookstores, and quiet residential streets.

I like walks without a fixed destination. When I enter an unfamiliar side street and notice signs, windows, tree shadows, and the pace of people, the city becomes more specific.

### Small scenes

- A morning train station where everyone moves quickly, yet the order does not feel tense.
- The quiet smell of paper inside a small bookstore.
- A window seat in a cafe, good for writing a few lines without conclusions.

Sometimes travel is not about collecting places. It is about using a different speed to observe life.
