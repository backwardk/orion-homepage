## 关于极简主义的一些思考

我理解的极简主义，不是把所有东西都变少，而是让重要的东西更清楚。

如果一个空间、工具或计划只是看起来很空，却没有让行动更容易，那它并不真正简洁。真正的简洁应该降低判断成本，让注意力自然回到重要的事情上。

### 少不是目的

- 少一些无效选择。
- 少一些临时决定。
- 少一些只是为了证明自己很忙的安排。

### 清晰才是

好的设计和好的生活秩序有相似之处：它们都不急着展示自己，而是在需要的时候稳定地支撑你。

极简不是冷淡，也不是克制到没有温度。它应该是一种更温和的清晰。

## A Few Thoughts on Minimalism

Minimalism, to me, is not about making everything fewer. It is about making what matters clearer.

If a space, tool, or plan only looks empty but does not make action easier, it is not truly simple. Real simplicity should reduce the cost of judgment and return attention to what matters.

### Less is not the goal

- Fewer ineffective choices.
- Fewer temporary decisions.
- Fewer plans made only to prove that we are busy.

### Clarity is

Good design and good personal order have something in common: they do not rush to display themselves, but they support you steadily when needed.

Minimalism should not feel cold. It can be a warmer kind of clarity.
